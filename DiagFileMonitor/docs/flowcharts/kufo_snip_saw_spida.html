KUFO SNIP SAW // MANUAL SAW // OP GUIDE REV 1.0

Kufo Snip Saw

MANUAL · NO SERVOS · THNTD · GUARD SWITCH · ON/OFF SWITCH · AIR CYLINDER · END OF STROKE AUTO-RETRACT

E-STOP CIRCUIT — MUST BE COMPLETE BEFORE SAW BLADE CAN OPERATE:
The pneumatic E-Stop circuit must be fully made before the saw blade can rise. All conditions must be satisfied:
  · Machine has power
  · E-Stop button is released (not pressed)
  · Guard switch NC contact is made (guard correctly closed)
  · ON/OFF switch is ON
If any condition is not met — saw blade will not rise.
GUARD SWITCH (NC contact):
The guard switch is wired normally closed (NC). When the guard is correctly closed, the NC contact is made — the pneumatic circuit is complete and the saw blade is permitted to rise. If the guard is open, the NC contact is broken — the circuit is interrupted and the saw blade cannot rise even if THNTD buttons are pressed.
END OF STROKE AUTO-RETRACT:
The end of stroke valve automatically resets the 5-port directional control valve when the air cylinder reaches full extension. This causes the saw blade to retract back down automatically after each cut — the operator does not need to do anything to bring the blade back down.
ON/OFF SWITCH — SAW BLADE:
A separate ON/OFF switch (NO/NC/COM) controls power to the saw blade motor. The blade must be switched ON before cuts can be made.

Immediately interrupts the pneumatic circuit — saw blade will not rise. If mid-cut, air cylinder retracts (spring return on 5-port valve). Release E-Stop button and close guard to restore circuit before resuming.
